Why this exists

This guide restructures the book into small, focused pages — the same way FastAPI's documentation breaks its tutorial into bite-sized, cross-linked sections. Easy material (the absolute basics of syntax) is kept brief; hard material (scope and closures, prototypal inheritance, the event loop, generators, proxies) gets full explanations, diagrams-in-text, and multiple worked examples so the concepts actually stick.
